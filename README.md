## Overview

Vorace is a Créajeux school project developed over two months by four programmers and four artists. You play as a pirate who has lost everything and sets out to reclaim what once belonged to him.

The game mixes fast combat, deck-building and procedural generation. At the beginning of a run, players select cards from a larger pool and combine effects such as summoned demons and lightning strikes.

## Core Experience

- Build a run from a selection of cards and combine their effects.
- Move and attack through procedurally assembled maps.
- Improve the character through a run-based upgrade system.
- Fight enemies while managing positioning, attacks and summoned effects.

## My Contribution

I worked on the main player systems and procedural content:

- Implemented player movement and attacks.
- Built the spritesheet animation workflow used by the player.
- Developed the upgrade system used during each run.
- Worked on interactions between the player, enemies and other entities.
- Contributed to the tile-based procedural map generator.
- Collaborated with programmers and artists throughout production.

## Technical Focus

Vorace was an important introduction to lower-level game programming. Without a modern engine providing gameplay architecture, the team had to manage game states, entities, rendering, animation and procedural generation directly through code.

The project strengthened my experience with:

- C gameplay programming
- SFML rendering and input
- Procedural tile-based generation
- Spritesheet animation
- Data-driven upgrades
- Multidisciplinary teamwork
